## What the result means

The intended winner changed with the job in both comparisons. All four aggregate ratings stayed within the ten-point tolerance declared before this fresh run. Thirty-one of 40 expected criterion levels matched exactly; all nine disagreements were one level apart. This is 77.5% exact agreement with the developer, not a claim of 77.5% hiring accuracy.

The factory candidate was seven points above the expected group-job rating. In particular, manual checks, supplier coordination, a backup owner and explaining cash to an owner received more credit than the written related-experience anchors suggested. These need recruiter review and calibration. Some expected labels are also debatable: the multinational profile did not state on-time reporting or how its reports were used, so the AI's lower month-end level has a defensible reason. The answer key was retained so these disagreements remain visible.

The adaptive conversations followed different stories and safely left unknown numbers unanswered. The multinational conversation unnecessarily asked for job preferences even though the input flag said they were set. Both conversations sometimes pursued quantities that were not supplied. These are remaining interview-quality limitations. Each four-turn sample ends with an unanswered follow-up; it is a bounded sample rather than a completed candidate onboarding.

The first job-interview pass criticised the hands-on candidate for lacking group reporting. That is irrelevant to the hands-on job. The production fix supplies the exact approved criterion and anchors to the interview prompt, with a new prompt version. All four job interviews in this report were rerun using that correction. Ranking assessments were retained because their matching instructions and calculation were unchanged. The explanatory per-answer marks remain model judgements; only the fixed criterion calculation sets ranking.

These use the same live provider, prompts and schemas as the app in an isolated Railway function. They do not exercise newly created candidate logins through every live UI screen. The approved facts were prewritten; a mock responder could select only existing fact IDs or say no example was shared. The profiles are known regression fixtures, not unseen or recruiter-approved validation cases.
